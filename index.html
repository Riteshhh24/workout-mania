📷 Laptop Camera Black Screen Fix

If permissions are granted but the camera video is still black or dark, check these 4 common laptop hardware & system causes:

1. 🔒 Physical Privacy Slider (Shutter)

Most modern laptops (Lenovo ThinkPad/IdeaPad, HP, Dell, Asus, Acer) have a small sliding mechanical switch right on top of the webcam lens. Slide it left or right to uncover the lens.

2. ⌨️ Keyboard Camera Toggle Key

Many laptops have a physical function key that disables the camera sensor. Try pressing Fn + F8, Fn + F10, or Fn + F6 (look for a key with a camera icon crossed out).

3. ⚙️ Windows Privacy Settings

Open Windows Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera. Ensure "Camera access" is ON and "Let desktop apps / browser access your camera" is ON.

4. 🔴 Another App Locking the Camera

If Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Skype, or Windows Camera App is open in the background, close them so your browser can access the camera stream.
